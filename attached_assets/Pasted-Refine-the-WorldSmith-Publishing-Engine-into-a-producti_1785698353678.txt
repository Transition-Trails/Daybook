Refine the WorldSmith Publishing Engine into a production cockpit.

The compiler experience is now strong. The next goal is to evolve the page from an "asset details" screen into a publishing workspace.

The user should always understand:

• Where am I?
• What am I working on?
• What should I do next?
• How does this asset affect the rest of the production pipeline?

Do not redesign the page from scratch.

Preserve the existing architecture.

Reorganize the experience around publishing.
