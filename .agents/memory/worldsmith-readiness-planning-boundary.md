---
name: WorldSmith readiness planning boundary
description: Intentional separation of Kanban planning lanes from authoritative editorial states
---

Readiness Kanban lanes for Canon Records, Storylines, and beats are planning metadata, not an editorial approval or publication workflow. “Ready” must never implicitly accept Canon, approve a storyline, or advance Production Spec status.

**Why:** The existing entities have separate governed statuses, and combining their statuses with planning progress would let routine board moves appear to grant authority they do not have.

**How to apply:** Maintain independent readiness assignments for each world-owned entity; verify the parent world when changing a beat; keep the Production Specs pipeline board distinct. The MCP planning surface uses its own explicitly consented readiness read/write scopes rather than inheriting Canon or editorial grants.
