Important distinction

Words such as realistic, plausible, tactile, material, detailed, natural light, wood grain, glass, brass, botanical specimen describe subject/material authenticity. They must not override the rendering medium.

“Realistic botanicals” means botanically credible illustrations, not photographic botanicals.

“Visible wood grain” means illustrated wood grain rendered through pigment/linework, not a photographically rendered wooden surface.

“Soft greenhouse daylight” describes illumination within an illustration, not photographic lighting.
