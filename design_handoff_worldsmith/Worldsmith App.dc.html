SUMMARY
The House of Ashcroft is both a family lineage and a durable Wychcombe institution whose influence extends through property, reputation, records, and inherited obligations.
DETAILS
Authority rests less in title than in custody. The House holds ledgers, correspondence and keys that other families need and cannot obtain, and its standing is maintained by inherited obligations that are rarely written down.
