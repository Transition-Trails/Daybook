---
name: Canon-grounded solo play
description: Product direction and ownership boundaries for store-owner story play grounded in WorldSmith Canon.
---

Use a guided Prompt Deck with one limited consequential branch as the first release. Reuse Stories, Acts, Encounters, Journal Prompts, and Canon links rather than creating a parallel narrative model.

**Why:** This provides a meaningful story experience with the lowest rules burden and clearest Canon-safety boundary. A full branching graph or persistent journal system adds more replayability but depends on substantially more session and state contracts.

Foundation Canon remains immutable to store owners. Play first produces private discoveries; owners may save owner-scoped contributions and explicitly submit proposals for editorial approval. Every derived scene and record retains provenance to its source Canon records and story moment.

WorldSmith also has a membership-gated Discord community where members and collaborators discuss, interpret, and help shape the world live. Community discussion may inform foundational Canon, but it never establishes Canon directly or bypasses editorial review. Keep Community discussion, Store workspace contributions, and Editorial Canon as three explicit scopes.

WorldSmith stories and Daybook form a provenance-aware product flywheel: story moments generate platform-level asset suggestions; editors choose and create reusable notebooks, ephemera, washi, and other physical or digital pieces; the library publishes them for Daybook and community use; usage can then inspire more story activity. Suggestions are inputs to the platform queue, not automatically published products.

The participation model must be world-agnostic. Use a shared interaction system across genres, while each world supplies its own presentation data, visual identity, Canon fragments, signals, and asset vocabulary. The recurring hook is: choose a world, encounter a signal, add an interpretation, see community use, and influence what the platform makes next.

Authors define reusable world atmosphere through author-owned voice direction and visual grammar. ElevenLabs-ready voice briefs can capture narration qualities, sample lines, pacing, and modes; HeyGen-ready visual briefs can capture materials, motifs, light, and color. Generated media follows these briefs and retains provenance; provider output never replaces authorship or establishes Canon.

World creation should begin with genre-aware seeds and connected, progressive prompts rather than a blank-page lore form. Prompts should help authors define central pressure, rules, secrets, relationships, meaning, materials, voice, and visual grammar, then turn the answers into reusable world context.

Romantasy is supported as a safe-for-work genre centered on emotional stakes, romantic tension, devotion, danger, and wonder. Keep this as a quiet Terms-of-Service and reusable world-tone boundary rather than dominant warning copy; generated scenes, media, and community activity inherit it.

Plan the MVP as four dependent phases: foundations, first play, belonging, and atmosphere. The working estimate is 7–13 focused weeks only when existing WorldSmith accounts, Canon, Daybook, and editorial tools are extended; moderation, provenance, permissions, and review are foundational rather than deferred polish.

**How to apply:** Before production implementation, define tenant-safe narrative routes, contribution ownership and statuses, provenance, session/resume state, choices, editorial promotion, Discord membership entitlements, community roles, moderation, story-signal suggestion generation, platform asset approval/creation, library publishing, Daybook handoff, world-level genre/theme presentation, progressive genre-aware prompt contracts, author-owned voice/visual contracts, media permissions, storage, usage tracking, provider integrations, and SFW content enforcement. Never expose platform-editorial routes directly to store owners, treat Discord messages as approved Canon, publish generated asset suggestions without editorial action, let generated media bypass author and editorial review, or permit explicit sexual content.
